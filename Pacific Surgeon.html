Pacific Surgeon

Hospital ship MERCY STAR. Leyte Gulf, October 1944.

Casualties arrive by landing craft all day and all night. Pick who to operate on, work with your tools, and keep the ship stocked while the war goes on outside the window.

How to play

The ship

  • Each shift is 12 hours: day, then night. Casualties keep arriving and the sea war never stops.
  • Pick a patient from the triage deck. Red tags die fastest. Fees depend on the quality of your surgery.
  • Spend fees in the supply room on stock and ship upgrades. Keep your reputation above zero.
  • Shells landing near the ship shake the table. Watch for the INCOMING warning. At night the lights can fail.

In the operating room

  • Click a wound marker on the body map to zoom into that part. Use keys 1 to 7 to switch tools.
  • Syringe: anaesthetic before cutting, or plasma when blood volume drops.
  • Scalpel: press at the green dot and drag along the line. Amputations: cut across the limb inside the green band.
  • Forceps: hold on a fragment until it comes out. Shears: hold on the bone to snap it.
  • Disinfectant: hold over a wound until it turns amber. Do it last, right before closing.
  • Needle and thread: click each glowing ring to stitch. Bandage: dress the wound.

Closing wounds

  • Graze: disinfect, then bandage. That closes it.
  • Laceration: disinfect, stitch, then bandage.
  • Shrapnel: open, pull every fragment, disinfect, stitch, bandage.
  • Crushed limb: cut, shears, disinfect the stump, stitch, bandage.
  • A bandage on an unstitched wound only slows the bleeding. Closing over unclean tissue raises infection risk.
