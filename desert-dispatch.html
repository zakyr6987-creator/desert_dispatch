Sd.Kfz. 222 · North Africa

Desert Dispatch

A sealed dispatch has to reach the generals at the forward headquarters before 18:00. The staff map gives only a rough grid for the HQ. Everything else is blank sand. Drive out, uncover the map as you go, and look for abandoned depots and wrecked supply trucks to keep the armoured car running.

  • The engine heats up on slopes, at full throttle and at midday. Past 118°C it stalls. Open the hood (H) or pour coolant (C) while stopped.
  • Stop beside a site and hold still to search it. Oases give free coolant.
  • W A S D or arrows drive, Space brakes, M opens the map and pauses, drag to look around.
